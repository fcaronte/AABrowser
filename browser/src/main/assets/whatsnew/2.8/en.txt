• Other Apps (Shizuku): your phone's apps on the car screen, in a CarWeb tab or in CarWebWide, with touch, a side bar and the keyboard. Split view with the browser (65/35, right or left); when the app stops playing the browser resumes.
• Bluetooth mouse and keyboard of the phone to control the car screen (Settings > Other Apps): pointer, clicks, wheel and browser-style shortcuts.
• The same sidebar in CarWeb and CarWebWide, with the buttons of your choice; in CarWebWide the top-right button shows or hides it.
• One Settings menu for CarWeb and CarWebWide, with sections that open and close. The CarWebWide home looks like CarWeb's and the favorites can be edited from there too.
• Desktop or mobile is chosen per site (icon) and per favorite, with zoom: the general switch is gone.
• TV: if a channel stops without you pausing it, it resumes by itself; in fullscreen a row of channels to jump straight to the one you want. Favorites are always in view in TV, Radio and Podcasts.
• Radio: animated effects (bars, wave, circle) and a message when a station does not start or stays silent.
• Tab bar buttons of your choice, Audio boost, and Fit, Fill, Stretch also on YouTube for computers.
• Updates and links keep working when the GitHub repository is renamed to CarWeb.

Apps that hide their windows (banks, Poste) show if the phone allows it; hardware-DRM video (Netflix) stays black.
