• Other Apps: with Shizuku you can use your phone's apps on the car screen, in a CarWeb tab or in CarWebWide, with touch, a side bar and the keyboard. Starred apps are tiles on the home page.
• While an app is on the car screen the phone stays awake behind a black "CarWeb attivo" screen; if you lock it, it wakes up again by itself, or with a tap on the car screen.
• Tab bar buttons of your choice: which ones, in what order and how many show together; the others are one swipe away.
• Desktop or mobile site: the choice now really sticks, also on YouTube and on sites that change address (m. / www.), and the site-specific fixes come back when you return to mobile.
• YouTube fullscreen: Fit, Fill and Stretch also work on the desktop site.
• TV in fullscreen: a tap on the video also shows a small panel to change channel.
• Radio: the music player's animated visual effects (bars, wave, circle), from the button next to the controls.
• A video whose sound the phone cannot decode says so and suggests opening it with the app you normally use, from Other Apps.

Apps that hide their windows (banks, Poste) show if the phone allows it; otherwise they stay black and CarWeb tells you so. Hardware-DRM video (Netflix) stays black: a virtual screen cannot show it.
